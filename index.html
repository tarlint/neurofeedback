What is Neurofeedback?

Neurofeedback, also known as EEG biofeedback, is a non-invasive form of brain training that uses real-time information about brain activity. During sessions, sensors read brainwave patterns and provide feedback through visual and auditory cues.

At New Paths for Health in Missoula, neurofeedback training begins with a QEEG brain map and a personalized plan. Sessions are designed around your goals, symptoms, and nervous system patterns.

Common Reasons People Search for Neurofeedback

  • Improved focus and attention
  • Better emotional regulation
  • Enhanced sleep quality
  • Reduced anxiety and stress
  • Increased cognitive performance
  • Non-invasive and drug-free
